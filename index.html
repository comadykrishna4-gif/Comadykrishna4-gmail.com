WHAT IS KON BANEGA KING?

Kon Banega King is an adventure-based reality challenge where courage, intelligence, stamina and determination are tested.

Contestants will face unexpected challenges, difficult situations, physical tasks and mental battles. Every challenge brings them one step closer to becoming the ultimate King.

No shortcuts. No easy victory. Only the strongest, smartest and most determined contestant can reach the crown.
